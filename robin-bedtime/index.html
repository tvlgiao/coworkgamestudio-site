Robin Sleep & Bedtime Stories

Calm bedtime stories, read aloud. 35 original stories told by a warm narrator, with words that glow as they are read, for your child's bedtime.

  • iPhone & iPad: App Store
  • Android: Google Play
Get it onGoogle Play

What is Robin?

Robin is a bedtime story app for the end of the day. Pick a story, turn the lights down and let a warm narrator read it aloud while your child follows along or drifts off. Each story takes about 6 to 11 minutes, and there are over 4 hours of stories in all.

It is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad (rated 4+) and on Google Play for Android. It has no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and no subscription.

Screenshots

Features

  • 35 original stories – each about 6 to 11 minutes, over 4 hours in total, read aloud by a warm narrator.
  • Read-along – words glow as they are read, and you can change the text size.
  • Five worlds – Forest, Ocean, Stars, Animals and Family.
  • Hand-painted cover art for every story.
  • Sleep timer with a soft fade. Audio keeps playing with the screen locked.
  • Playback speed control, plus Favorites, Read Later and Continue listening.
  • Works fully offline – no Wi-Fi or mobile data needed.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no analytics.
  • 8 stories are free. A one-time unlock opens the rest. There is no subscription.
