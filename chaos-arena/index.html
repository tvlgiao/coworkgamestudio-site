Chaos Arena: Push Party

A short, silly party brawler for 2 to 8 players. Everyone stands on one floor that keeps falling apart. Push, dash and grab items, and try not to be the one who drops off. The last player standing wins, and a match lasts about 90 seconds.

What is Chaos Arena?

Chaos Arena is a party game you can pick up in seconds. Every match drops 2 to 8 players onto one arena. You have a push, a dash and whatever item you find in a crate, and the floor itself is working against everyone. Knock the others off, stay on your feet, and be the last one left.

Play with friends in a private room, jump into a public match, or practise against bots on your own, even offline. The game is out on the App Store for iPhone and iPad and on Google Play for Android, and plays free in your browser at chaosarena.coworkgamestudio.com.

How to play

  • Push – shove whoever is in front of you.
  • Dash – throw yourself forward much harder. It needs two seconds to come back, so a missed dash can leave you open.
  • Items – crates appear on the floor. The glove makes your next push knock people much further, the shield blocks one hit, and the banana peel goes down behind you for someone else to slip on.
  • Stay on the floor – the arena changes as the match goes on. Whoever is still standing at the end wins.

On a phone or tablet, drag on the left half of the screen to move and tap Push, Dash and Item on the right. On a keyboard, move with WASD or the arrow keys, push with Space or J, dash with Shift or K and use an item with E. The first time you play, a short guided match shows you everything in under a minute.

Features

Five arenas, five kinds of trouble

ArenaWhat makes it tricky
The crumbling floorEdge tiles flash, crack and drop away in waves, each one closer to the middle.
The rooftopBig fans blow everyone sideways, so a light push near the wrong fan goes a long way.
The raftsThe floor is made of rafts that drift apart. Standing on the gap is a bad idea.
The kitchenOil slicks make you slide and steam vents throw you around.
The marketRunaway carts roll across the stalls and take out whoever is in the way.

New in the browser: two more arenas, two more items

The web version already has the next update, and the apps get it in their next release.

  • The conveyor factory – five conveyor belts carry everyone along, the outer ones straight toward the drop, and the middle belt flips direction every few seconds after a warning.
  • The lazy Susan – a giant turntable spins the players around, reverses after a warning, and carries plates you can hide behind or bounce off.
  • Pillow – three wide swings that knock back everyone in front of you.
  • Water balloon – throw it four tiles ahead: the splash knocks people back and leaves a slippery puddle.
  • Bots and room rules – play against easy, normal or hard bots, and set how many items appear and whether a match lasts 60, 90 or 120 seconds.

Four characters

Belly Boss, Auntie Curls, Propeller Kid and Uncle Wrench each have their own look and push animation. They all play the same, so you pick who you want to be, not a set of stats.

Friends, strangers or bots

Play with friends opens a private room with a six-letter code: send the code or share the invite link. Play now puts you in a public room, and bots fill empty seats so a match always has at least four players. Play vs bots lets you practise on your own, offline too.

Levels and a daily challenge

Every match earns XP, you level up as you play, and each day brings a small challenge such as finishing in the top two twice.

14 languages

English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Indonesian, Thai, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. The game picks your device's language and you can switch in Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free to play. The mobile app shows an ad between some matches, never during a match, and has a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. Pick a nickname and play. Your level and progress are kept on your device.

How many players can play together?

2 to 8 in one match. Public rooms fill empty seats with bots, and you can play against bots alone.

How do I play with my friends?

Tap Play with friends to open a private room, then send your friends the six-letter code or the invite link. Everyone who joins plays in the same match.

What if someone uses a rude nickname?

Nicknames go through a filter. In Settings you can hide other players' names, and Report a player sends us an email so we can look into it.

How do I report a bug or ask for help?

Email support@coworkgamestudio.com with your device and what happened. See also our contact page and the devlog post Meet Chaos Arena.
