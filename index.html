Drone Sim

An FPV drone flight simulator with realistic quad physics. Learn to hover on a quiet meadow, thread the rings of a neon night loft, race AI pilots through a city at dusk, or fly free across an endless generated countryside.

How we make games

01

Real feel first

Our drones fly on a physics model of a real quad, with the flight modes and Betaflight-style rates that real pilots use. What you learn in the game carries over to the real thing.

02

Easy to start

A guided tutorial, an assisted GPS mode and gentle default rates let a complete beginner fly in minutes. When you are ready, the training wheels come off one at a time.

03

Play anywhere

Web first: open a link and fly on a computer, phone or tablet with no download. The same game is on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac and on the Meta Horizon Store for Quest, with Google Play next, with touch, keyboard, gamepad, RC transmitter and VR controls.
