Godreign: the boss calls the Tide, a new shop and clearer fights

By Cowork Game Studio · Published 9 November 2026 · Godreign: War of Four

Hi everyone,

A new update on Godreign: War of Four, our team game about a great war between four god-level civilisations. Four teams of three fight on one map at the same time. If you are new here, the first look explains how a match works.

This round changed three things: what sets off the Tide, how you shop, and how easy it is to follow a fight. Everything below is work in progress, including the picture above.

The boss calls the Tide

The Tide is what pushes a four-way match toward an end. In the first version it arrived on a timer. At a set point in the match it started to press on every team at once, no matter what anyone had done.

That timer is gone. The Tide now comes from the boss in the centre of the map. The team that defeats the boss chooses which rival team the Tide marches on.

So the Tide is now something a team earns, not something that just happens. Every team has a reason to care about the boss fight, even a team that is not trying to take it, because the winner decides where the Tide goes next. In the first look we said that with four teams you always have to ask who else is watching. Now you also have to ask who the winner will pick.

For the team that takes the boss it is a real decision: which of the other three teams should the Tide hit?

A new shop

The shop was rebuilt. Items are now sorted into categories, so you can go straight to boots, armour or magic resist instead of scrolling one long list.

Every hero has a recommended build. It sits at the top of the shop, so a new player can buy the right items without knowing them yet. If you want, you can turn on auto-buy, and the game buys the recommended items for you when you are at your fountain.

Item names and descriptions are now in English too, so the shop reads properly in both languages.

Easier to read a fight

With twelve heroes on one map, a big fight can turn into a blur. A lot of this round went into making it clear who is who and what just happened.

Match intro. Every match now opens with a 10-second overview of the battlefield. The camera shows the whole map, and the objectives drop onto it: the guardians and the boss. By the time you take control you know where everything is.

Team rings. Every hero has a ring under it in its team colour. In a crowded fight you can tell at a glance which of the four teams a hero belongs to.

Faces on the minimap. The minimap now shows hero faces, so you can see who is where, not just that someone is there.

When a hero falls. Heroes now say a line when they fall. Defeated heroes and minions now collapse and fade out instead of vanishing, so you can see that something died and where.

End-of-match stats. After a match there is a full stats table for every hero on every team: level, kills, deaths and assists, gold, damage to heroes, damage taken, healing and shields, damage to structures, minions and monsters, and items. Each team shows its rank, and teams that were knocked out show when it happened.

That is it for this round. We will post again when there is something new to show. The rest of our projects are on the games page.
