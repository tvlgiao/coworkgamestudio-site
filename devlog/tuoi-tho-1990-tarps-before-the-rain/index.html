Tuổi Thơ 1990: tarps before the rain, sacks the next morning

By Cowork Game Studio · Published 16 November 2026 · Tuổi Thơ 1990

Hi everyone,

Another short note from chapter 4 of Tuổi Thơ 1990, the chapter about drying the rice harvest in the yard. This week was about small moments: covering the rice before a sudden rain, and tying it into sacks the next morning. Everything here is work in progress.

Before the rain

When the storm comes in, the girl pulls tarps over the rice. The tarps now fold neatly inside the yard and go over the rice. The picture above is a test frame from that moment.

We are still tuning the size of the drying patches, so that the whole harvest fits under the tarps before the rain arrives.

The next morning

After the rain night there is now a short card that moves the story to the next morning, instead of jumping straight there.

In the morning the rice goes into sacks. The hint for tying a sack now sits on the ground around the sack, so it no longer crosses the girl's arms, and the tied sacks are laid out on the cement.

Next

We are also fixing the raking so the girl walks around the porch posts instead of through them. We will post again when there is more to show.
