Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe: a new update and a faster start in the browser

By Cowork Game Studio · Published 16 November 2026 · Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe

Hi everyone,

A short one this week for Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe, our coffee cart and garden builder on a hillside of pines. Since the game came out on the App Store and Google Play, the work has been on updates and on how quickly you get to play.

Out now

  • iPhone and iPad: version 1.0.1 is out on the App Store.
  • Android: the latest update is out on Google Play.

If you already have the game, your store will offer the update.

A faster start in the browser

The browser version now opens with a start screen and a PLAY button. One tap on PLAY starts the music and the sound and opens the game, all on that same tap.

Next we keep polishing. You can find the game on iPhone, iPad, Android and in your browser from the game page.

See you up the hill.
