Meet Godreign: War of Four

By Cowork Game Studio · Published 2 November 2026 · Godreign: War of Four

Hi everyone,

This is the first time we talk about Godreign: War of Four. It is a team game where four teams of three heroes fight on one map, all at the same time. It is early and we only have internal builds in Unity, so there is nothing to play yet. We just wanted to say hello and show what we have so far, rough edges included. Everything in the pictures and the clip below is work in progress, including the picture above.

The world

The map is a floating sky citadel, left behind by an ancient civilisation of alien gods. Think sci-fi meets myth: old stone, glowing crystal and a lot of sky around it.

Minions and monsters

This week the lane minions and the jungle monsters got real animated 3D models. They share one colour palette, so minions take their team colour and every monster species has its own colour.

Heroes

Heroes now face their target while they attack, and they run more smoothly. Small things, but it feels much better. We are also reworking the ultimate of one hero, Astra. It is turning into a dash and shoot across several positions. That one is still in progress, so expect it to change.

Language

The interface comes in Vietnamese and English. It follows the language of your device and you can switch it any time.

Next up

We will post again when there is something new to show.
