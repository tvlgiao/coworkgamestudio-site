Coffee Street: Dalat Hills, a bigger build mode and a story you can hear

By Cowork Game Studio · Published 9 November 2026 · Coffee Street: Dalat Hills

Hi everyone,

Another update on Coffee Street: Dalat Hills, our coffee cart and garden builder on a Da Lat hillside. Last time the whole story became playable. This week was about what you do between the story beats: building, moving things around and making the hill feel nicer to walk on. The story also got a voice. In short: a bigger build mode, a kiosk you can move, narrated chapters, new characters and a long list of small fixes around the hill.

Everything below is work in progress. The picture above is the greenhouse, which now sits on a brick base wall.

Build mode

Build mode got the most work this round.

Floors. There is a new Floors group. You can paint floors with a brush or drag a box to fill an area. The choices are wood tiles, patterned tiles, slate and gravel.

Paths. You can draw paths freehand now, and lay stepping stones the same way. The stepping stones that come with the cart can be cleared too, so the ground around the cart is yours to plan.

Choose many. This one is for tidying up a full garden. With Choose many you drag one box across the map, even across terrace levels, and the items inside are picked: tables, benches, flowers, lamps, even a koi pond. The cart and stairs stay put. Then you store them all or sell them all in one go.

The build bar. The build bar was redone: the item groups sit in one strip you can swipe, next to a small fixed set of tools, all with painted icons.

Move your kiosk. You can now pick up the coffee cart and set it down anywhere guests can walk. That includes the upper terraces, once you have built stairs up to them.

While you move the cart, a small chip under it shows how much extra walking time the new spot adds for guests, so you can weigh a nice view against a longer walk for a cup.

Story and characters

Narration. The opening scene and every chapter now have narration. A narrator reads the story in Vietnamese or English, and subtitles are always on, so you can follow along with the sound off.

Each chapter card has a Replay button. If you skipped a chapter's narration, or just want to hear it again, tap Replay on its card.

Guests. Nine new guest characters come up the hill for coffee now.

Your owner. The owner, that is you, can be male or female. You can change outfits: an apron, a sweater, a raincoat or an áo dài. And hats: a felt hat, a nón lá, a beanie or a cap.

Idle poses got a pass too. When characters stand around, they now stand naturally instead of holding a stiff pose.

A smoother hill

A lot of small fixes landed this week.

  • Plots. Plots are now evenly sized on every level, and the terrace edges run straight.
  • Greenhouse. It sits on a brick base wall instead of straight on the grass.
  • Walking. You can walk straight across the joins between plots, and nobody walks through flowers or deck rails any more.
  • Seating. Seated guests sit at the right height, and their cups sit on the table.
  • Download size. The mobile download is much smaller.

Next we keep polishing. We will post again when there is something new to show. If you missed the last update, the whole story is playable covers chapters 4 and 5, endless mode and the staff. Everything else we make is on the games page.

You can play it now in your browser: the public beta is open to everyone, no sign-in needed.

See you up the hill.
