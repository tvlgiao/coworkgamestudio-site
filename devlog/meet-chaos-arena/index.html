Meet Chaos Arena: push your friends off the floor

By Cowork Game Studio · Published 22 November 2026 · Chaos Arena

Hi everyone,

Say hello to Chaos Arena, a short and silly party game for 2 to 8 players. Everyone stands on one floor. You push, you dash, you grab an item, and you try not to be the one who falls off. The last player standing wins. A match lasts 90 seconds, so there is always time for one more.

It runs in the browser on a computer, a phone or a tablet, in English and Vietnamese. You can play Chaos Arena now.

How a match works

You have three moves. Push shoves whoever is in front of you. Dash throws you forward much harder, but it needs two seconds to come back, so a missed dash can leave you open. Crates also appear on the floor with items inside: the glove and the shield work on their own, and Use item drops a banana peel.

The floor does not stay still. On the first arena, tiles near the edge start to flash, then crack, then drop away in waves, and each wave eats closer to the middle. If nobody has been pushed off by the end, there is not much floor left to stand on.

Five arenas, five kinds of trouble

Each arena adds its own way to lose your footing:

  • The crumbling floor. The classic: the edge falls away in waves until only the middle is left.
  • The rooftop. Big fans blow everyone sideways, so a light push near the wrong fan goes a long way.
  • The rafts. The floor is made of rafts that drift apart. Standing on the gap is a bad idea.
  • The kitchen. Oil slicks make you slide and steam vents throw you around.
  • The market. Runaway carts roll across the stalls and take out whoever is in the way.

Four characters and three items

Pick one of four characters: Belly Boss, Auntie Curls, Propeller Kid and Uncle Wrench. Each one has a look and a push animation of its own. They all play the same, so the choice is about who you want to be, not about stats.

Crates on the floor hold one of three items:

  • Glove: your next push knocks people much further.
  • Shield: blocks one hit.
  • Banana peel: drop it behind you and let someone else slip.

Play with friends, or with bots

Play with friends opens a private room with a six-letter code. Send the code, or share the invite link, which also shows your name to the friend who opens it. Private rooms wait a little longer before the countdown so everyone has time to join.

Play now puts you in a public room. If there are not enough people, bots fill the room so a match always has at least four players. You can also choose Play vs bots to practise on your own, even offline.

The first time you play, a short guided match shows you push, dash and items in under a minute.

Levels and a daily challenge

Every match earns XP, and you level up as you go. Each day there is a small challenge, such as finishing in the top two twice.

Controls

  • Keyboard: move with WASD or the arrow keys, push with Space or J, dash with Shift or K, use an item with E.
  • Touch: drag on the left half of the screen to move, and tap the Push, Dash and Item buttons on the right.

The game picks English or Vietnamese from your device, and you can switch in Settings.

What comes next

We are watching how the first matches go: which arena people pick, where the bots are too easy or too hard, and how the game feels on phones. We will write about the changes here on the devlog. If you play, we would love to hear what made you laugh and what felt unfair.
