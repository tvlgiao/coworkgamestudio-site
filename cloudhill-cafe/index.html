Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe

Tiệm Cà Phê Đồi Mây Rừng Thông

A cozy game about running a little coffee cart and growing it into a garden café on a misty pine hill.

  • iPhone & iPad: App Store, coming soon
  • Android: Google Play, coming soon

About the game

Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe is the store name of the game we write about on our devlog as Coffee Street: Dalat Hills. It is set on a hillside in Da Lat, the town of pine forests, flower gardens and cool mornings in Viet Nam's Central Highlands, where a cup of coffee is part of the day.

You start with one small coffee cart next to a path. Passers-by stop, you brew their drinks and serve them while they wait. What you earn goes back into the hill around you: tables and stools so guests can sit down, flowers and pines, stone paths and fences wherever you want them, and staff to help. Baristas work the cart, a server carries drinks to seated guests and a musician plays on the stage.

Five chapters up the hill

As the cart gets busier, more of the hill opens up: a deck above the valley, then a little pine grove. Later chapters bring a flower greenhouse with a koi pond, and a hilltop with a sunset deck, a telescope and a stage. The story ends with the Hill Coffee Festival: the regulars gather on the hilltop, Tùng sings, and paper fireworks bloom over the valley.

After the story

An endless mode keeps the game going after the last chapter. You can buy lots along the street, work through street levels, serve seasonal drinks, take requests from a daily board and upgrade your roast, at your own pace.

Status

The game is in development. The whole story already plays from the first cup to the festival in our internal builds; audio and release preparation come next. You can already play it in your browser as a private beta: sign in with Google to request access. We post progress on the devlog: first look and the whole story is playable.

Privacy

The game has no account and keeps your progress on your device. Read the Privacy Policy (Chính sách bảo mật, tiếng Việt).
