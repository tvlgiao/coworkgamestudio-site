Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe

Tiệm Cà Phê Đồi Mây Rừng Thông

A cozy game about running a little coffee cart and growing it into a garden café on a misty pine hill. Brew for the guests who climb the hill, build the garden around them and play the story through five chapters to a coffee festival on the hilltop.

What is Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe?

The game is set on a hillside in Da Lat, the town of pine forests, flower gardens and cool mornings in Viet Nam's Central Highlands, where a cup of coffee is part of the day. You start with one small coffee cart next to a path. Passers-by stop, you brew their drinks and serve them while they wait, and what you earn goes back into the hill around you.

It is a slow, friendly game with no timer pushing you along. Play a few minutes between other things, or spend an evening planning the garden. In our first devlog posts it was called Coffee Street: Dalat Hills.

The game is out on the App Store for iPhone and iPad and on Google Play for Android, and you can play it free in your browser at cafesim.coworkgamestudio.com.

How to play

  • Brew and serve – guests walk up the path and order at the cart. Brew their drink and hand it over while they wait.
  • Build – spend what you earn on tables and stools so guests can sit down, flowers and pines, stone paths, fences and lamps. Place things one at a time, or paint floors and paths across a whole area.
  • Hire staff – baristas work the cart, a server carries drinks to seated guests and a musician plays on the stage.
  • Climb the hill – as the cart gets busier, more of the hill opens up, terrace by terrace, until you reach the hilltop.

Features

A garden you plan yourself

Paint floors with a brush or drag a box to fill an area with wood tiles, patterned tiles, slate or gravel. Draw paths freehand and lay stepping stones. With Choose many you drag one box across the map, even across terrace levels, and store or sell everything inside in one go.

Move the cart

Pick up the coffee cart and set it down anywhere guests can walk, including the upper terraces once you have built stairs up to them. A small chip shows how much extra walking time the new spot adds, so you can weigh a nice view against a longer walk for a cup.

A story you can hear

A narrator reads the opening and every chapter in Vietnamese or English, with subtitles always on, so you can follow along with the sound off. Each chapter card has a Replay button.

Guests and staff

Regulars and new faces come up the hill for coffee. Baristas, a server and a musician help you once the cafe gets busy.

After the story

An endless mode keeps the game going after the last chapter. Buy lots along the street, work through street levels, serve seasonal drinks, take requests from a daily board and upgrade your roast, at your own pace.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The game has no account and keeps your progress on your device. In the browser, progress is saved in your browser's storage; clearing the site's data removes it.

Which languages does it support?

English and Vietnamese, for the interface and the narrated story.

Can I play with the sound off?

Yes. Subtitles are always on, and every chapter can be replayed from its card.

What happens after the festival?

Endless mode opens: more lots along the street, seasonal drinks, daily requests and roast upgrades.

How do I report a bug or ask for help?

Email support@coworkgamestudio.com with your device and what happened. See also our contact page. Progress posts are on the devlog: the whole story is playable and a bigger build mode and a story you can hear.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy (Chính sách bảo mật, tiếng Việt).

The browser version needs no sign-in and keeps your game progress in your browser. It sets an anonymous feedback id cookie (first-party, 1 year, no personal data) so reports from one player can be grouped. Feedback you send is anonymous unless you add your email, and we keep it (and any picture you attach) for up to 90 days. To be removed, write to support@coworkgamestudio.com.
