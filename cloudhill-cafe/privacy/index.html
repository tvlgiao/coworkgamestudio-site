Privacy Policy: Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe

COWORK COMPANY LIMITED · Last updated 6 November 2026

English · Tiếng Việt

This policy covers the game Vietnam Cloudhill & Pines Cafe (Vietnamese: Tiệm Cà Phê Đồi Mây Rừng Thông, "the game") on iPhone, iPad and Android. The game is published by CÔNG TY TNHH COWORK (COWORK COMPANY LIMITED), tax code 0319684925 ("we", "us"). Our website has its own studio privacy policy.

1. Summary

The game has no account, no server of ours and no analytics. Your progress is stored only on your device. The free version shows ads through a third-party ad service, which is the only way data about you leaves your device. Versions of the game without ads contain no ad SDK and send nothing.

2. What stays on your device

The game writes these files in its own app folder. We never receive them. Deleting the game deletes them (your phone's own backup, such as iCloud or Google Auto Backup, may keep a copy under your own account).

  • Your progress: the café, items, money, chapter steps, settings (music, language, text size, weather), and the name you give your café. The name is only shown inside the game.
  • Your birth year, asked once to choose the right ad and privacy settings (section 5). It is never sent anywhere.
  • Your privacy choices (consent answers, the "do not sell or share" switch) and a note of which purchases you own.
  • Counters that limit how often an ad can appear (time played, when the last ad was shown).
  • A play log (simple events such as "served a coffee", the step reached, timestamps) kept in the app folder to help us fix balance problems. It is not uploaded. It leaves your device only if you choose to send it to us yourself.

3. Ads (free version)

The free version shows a full-screen ad at one natural break (when you tap Next day on the day report, after you have played for a few minutes), and you may choose to watch an optional rewarded ad for a small decoration. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay by Unity Technologies; the only ad network connected to it today is Unity Ads. If we add another network, we will list it here.

To show and measure ads, these services may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, app information, and your interactions with ads. They act as independent controllers of that data under their own privacy policies (Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy). We do not receive this data, and we receive no information about you from them.

  • Personalised ads are used only where the law allows and you agreed. On iOS, the system "Allow tracking" prompt decides. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) decides; see Google's privacy policy. In the United States you can switch on "Do not sell or share my information" in Settings > Privacy in the game.
  • If you decline, you still see ads, but not personalised ones.
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase removes all ads for good. Once ads are removed, no ad SDK is started and no consent questions are asked.

4. Purchases

Purchases (the "Remove ads" pack and the "Support the café" pack) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details or your store account. The game asks the store which purchases you own so that the Restore button works, and keeps that answer on your device.

5. Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children. To protect younger players it asks your birth year once. If you are under 13 (under 16 in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game switches to children's mode: no tracking prompt, no consent forms, ad requests are flagged as child-directed, and only non-personalised ads are shown. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and the birth year never leaves the device. Parents can also use the store's parental controls.

6. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, precise location, health data or payment details. The game has no chat, no social features and no user-generated content shared with others. We use no analytics and no crash-reporting service.

7. Your choices

  • In the game, Settings > Privacy: tap Ad privacy options to reopen the consent form (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or switch "do not sell or share my information" (United States).
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some phones Settings > Google > Ads): delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation.

8. Deleting your data

We hold no data about you on any server. The game has no account and does not upload your progress, so there is nothing on our side to export or delete, and no deletion request is needed for us.

Everything the game knows about you is in its folder on your device. To remove it:

  • Start over: on the title screen, choose New game. Your current progress is replaced by a new save.
  • Delete everything on iPhone or iPad: delete the app (touch and hold its icon, then Remove App > Delete App, or Settings > General > iPhone Storage > the game > Delete App). This removes your progress, birth year, privacy choices and play log.
  • Delete everything on Android: Settings > Apps > the game > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the game.
  • A copy may remain in your own device backup (iCloud, Google) until that backup is replaced or deleted.
  • Purchases you made stay with your Apple or Google account, so you can get them back with the Restore button after you reinstall.

For data held by the ad services in section 3, use their privacy pages linked above, or email us at support@coworkgamestudio.com and we will forward your request.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA and the UK under the GDPR, California and other US states, or Viet Nam under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the game keeps your data only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights yourself as described in sections 7 and 8; for anything else, contact us.

10. Transfers and retention

The ad services may process data outside your country; their policies describe the safeguards they use. We keep no personal data from the game, so we have no retention period of our own. If you email us, the studio privacy policy applies to your message.

11. Changes

We will update this page when the game changes and change the date at the top.
