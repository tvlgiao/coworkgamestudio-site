In development

These games are not out yet. Everything we show of them is work in progress and can still change. We post progress on our devlog first.

Tuổi Thơ 1990

A gentle memoir game about a seven-year-old girl growing up in the early 1990s in southern Viet Nam. Each chapter is a small slice of family life: chores in the yard, the rice harvest, the weather turning. In the chapter we are building now, the girl and her mother rake the drying rice, fill and tie the sacks, and cover everything with tarps when the rain comes. Chapter 2 is playable on our itch.io page, and the newer chapters are in a private browser beta.

Read more: drying the rice, work in progress.

Godreign: War of Four

A team game where four teams of three heroes fight on one map at the same time. The map is a floating sky citadel left behind by an ancient civilisation of alien gods: old stone, glowing crystal and a lot of sky. Lanes are full of minions, the jungle has monsters, and each hero has an ultimate ability that can turn a fight. It is early; the interface already comes in Vietnamese and English.

Read more: meet Godreign: War of Four. A private browser beta against bots is open now.

What comes next for Drone Sim

Drone Sim keeps growing based on what players tell us: new courses, more online features and more drones. The game guide covers every level, the controls and how to set up a real RC transmitter, and the devlog explains how the flight model, the online races and the Ha Long Bay and Hoi An courses were built.

Play in your browser

Both games run in the browser on a computer, phone or tablet. No download, no sign-up.
