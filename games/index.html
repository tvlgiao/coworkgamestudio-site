Drone Sim

FPV drone flight simulator. Realistic quad physics, three cameras, three flight modes, six levels from a training meadow to an endless generated world, and races against AI pilots from Novice to Pro.

Genre
Flight simulation, racing
Status
Out now: free in the browser, on the App Store (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and on the Meta Horizon Store (Quest); Android coming soon
Platforms
Web browser · iPhone, iPad & Mac · Meta Quest · Android (coming soon)
Controls
Touch, keyboard, gamepad, RC transmitter, Meta Quest Touch

What comes next

Our focus right now is bringing Drone Sim to Google Play and adding to it based on what players tell us: new courses, more online features and more drones. News about future games will be posted on our devlog first.
