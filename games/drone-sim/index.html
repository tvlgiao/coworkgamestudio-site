Drone Sim

An FPV drone flight simulator with realistic quad physics. Learn to hover, then race through rings in a neon night loft, a toy town, Hoi An by night, Ha Long Bay, a city at dusk, an alpine valley and an endless generated world, alone, against bots or online.

What is Drone Sim?

Drone Sim puts you at the sticks of an FPV racing quad: the small, fast drones that pilots fly through goggles showing a live view from the drone's camera. Unlike a camera drone, an FPV quad does not fly itself. It responds directly to the pilot's inputs, which is what makes it so fast and agile, and why it takes practice to fly well.

The game simulates that quad with a physics model built like a real one: motors, thrust, drag, momentum and a flight controller with the same flight modes and rate settings real pilots use. It can be a relaxed way to fly around and look at the scenery, a training tool before you buy your first real quad, or a racing game where you chase your best lap and beat AI opponents.

Drone Sim is free to play in a web browser on a computer, phone or tablet, with no download and no account. It is also on the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac and on the Meta Horizon Store for Meta Quest; Android is coming soon.

How to play

Your first launch opens the Training Field and a twelve-step tutorial: arm the motors, take off, hover, turn, fly in each direction, land, disarm, try the flight modes and cameras, and fly through your first ring. After that, the main menu offers these ways to fly:

  • Race – fly the level's ring course against the clock, alone or against AI pilots. Rings are numbered and the next one is highlighted, with a compass and minimap to guide you. The game keeps your best time and your split time at each ring.
  • Time Trial – race alone against the ghost of your own best lap.
  • Online – Quick match into a live race, create a private room with a six-character code for friends, or race the ghost of any pilot on the leaderboards. See Racing online.
  • Free Fly – no clock and no course. Explore the level, practise a manoeuvre, or just enjoy the flight.

Features

Built like a real quad

The drone has a physical model with real momentum. It tilts to move, sinks when you tilt without adding throttle, drifts through turns and bounces off walls. Crashes are part of learning, and a reset puts you back in the air in a second.

Three cameras

FPV shows the view from the drone's camera, the way racers fly. Line of sight puts you at the pilot spot, like standing at a real flying field; when the drone climbs high overhead the pilot steps back to keep it in view. Chase follows behind the quad and is the easiest view to learn with. Switch at any time.

Three flight modes

GPS holds height and position and brakes when you let go. Angle self-levels but leaves the throttle to you. Acro has no self-levelling at all: the stick sets the rotation rate, as on a real racing or freestyle quad. Read GPS, Angle and Acro explained for a full comparison.

Betaflight-style rates

Center sensitivity, max rate and expo per axis with live curves, Beginner, Intermediate and Pro presets, plus throttle mid, expo and limit. See Rates and expo explained.

AI opponents

Race against AI pilots at five skill levels: Novice, Rookie, Pilot, Ace and Pro. They fly the same quad as you; a higher tier simply flies it better, and the top two fly in Acro.

Frequently asked questions

Is Drone Sim really free?

Yes. The web version is free to play in full, with every level, and needs no account and no download. The App Store version is free with ads; the Meta Quest version is free to try, with a one-time purchase for the full game.

Which browsers and devices does it run on?

Drone Sim runs in current desktop and mobile browsers with WebGL support, on computers, phones and tablets. Graphics quality can be lowered in the settings for older devices.

Can I use my real RC transmitter?

Yes. A transmitter connected over USB that appears to the computer as a game controller can be used, with stick modes 1 to 4 and an axis setup screen to map its channels.

I have never flown a drone. Where should I start?

Play the tutorial on the Training Field in GPS mode with the chase camera, then read our getting started guide.

Does the game save my progress?

Your settings, best race times and ring split times are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing the site's data in the browser removes them.

Will it help me fly a real FPV drone?

It teaches the same skills: stick control, throttle management, orientation and Acro flying, with the same rate model as Betaflight. A simulator is not a substitute for learning local drone rules or flying safely, but it is a cheap way to practise before you risk a real quad.

How do I report a bug or ask for help?

Email support@coworkgamestudio.com with your device, browser and what happened. See our contact page.

Ready to fly?
