Player support

If something is not working in Drone Sim, email us and include:

  • the device and operating system (for example, "iPhone, iOS" or "Windows laptop");
  • the browser and its version if you play on the web;
  • your controller (touch, keyboard, gamepad model or RC transmitter);
  • the level, and what you were doing when the problem happened;
  • a screenshot or short video if you can.

Many common questions are answered in the Drone Sim guide and FAQ and in our devlog guides.
