Cowork Game Studio is a small independent studio in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. We make simulation games that are easy to start and deep enough to keep practising.

Our games

Drone Sim

An FPV drone flight simulator with realistic quad physics. Race through rings in a neon night loft, a city, an alpine valley, a toy town and endless generated worlds, or just fly free.

  • FPV, line-of-sight and chase cameras
  • GPS, Angle and Acro flight modes with Betaflight-style rates
  • Touch, keyboard, gamepad and RC transmitter controls
  • Race against AI pilots, from Novice to Pro
  • Meta Quest VR

About us

COWORK COMPANY LIMITED (Công ty TNHH Cowork) was founded in 2026 in Ho Chi Minh City. Cowork Game Studio is its games label. We build our games for the web first, so anyone can try them in a browser, and then bring them to phones and VR headsets.
