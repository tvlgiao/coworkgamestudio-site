Privacy Policy

COWORK COMPANY LIMITED · Effective 11 October 2026

This policy covers the website coworkgamestudio.com and its subdomains, including the web version of Drone Sim at dronesim.coworkgamestudio.com ("the sites"), operated by COWORK COMPANY LIMITED ("we", "us"). Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other contact details unless you email us. Game settings and records are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device.

Advertising

The free web versions of our games may show ads served by Google AdSense. Ads help us keep these games free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, through a consent message on the site. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link in the game.

For more information about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Hosting

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and ads shown to users identified as children are limited to non-personalised ads as required by Google's policies.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above.
