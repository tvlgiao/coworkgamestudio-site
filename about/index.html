About Cowork Game Studio

An independent game studio in Ho Chi Minh City, Viet Nam, and the games label of COWORK COMPANY LIMITED.

Who we are

Cowork Game Studio is the games label of COWORK COMPANY LIMITED, a company registered in Ho Chi Minh City on 20 August 2026. We are a small, independent team. We make the games we want to play ourselves: simulations that are honest about how things really move, but welcoming enough that anyone can pick them up.

Our first game is Drone Sim, an FPV drone flight simulator. It started from a simple question: how do you let someone feel what it is like to fly a racing quad without asking them to buy one, build one and crash it? The answer was a simulator that runs in a browser tab, teaches the basics step by step, and still behaves like a real quad when you turn the assists off.

Our mission

We want to make flying, and the skills behind it, accessible to everyone. In practice that means three things:

  • Real behaviour. Our physics, flight modes and settings follow the real hobby, so what you learn in our games is useful outside them.
  • A gentle start. Tutorials, assisted modes and sensible defaults, so no one gives up in the first five minutes.
  • Open access. We build for the web first, so a game is one link away on any computer, phone or tablet, and then bring it to the App Store, Google Play and Meta Quest.

How we fund our games

We want our games to stay free to try. The web version of Drone Sim is free and supported by advertising. The App Store version is free with ads between races, with a one-time purchase to remove them. The Meta Quest version is free to try, with a one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. We aim to keep ads out of the way of play, and our Privacy Policy explains how advertising cookies work and how to opt out of personalised ads.
