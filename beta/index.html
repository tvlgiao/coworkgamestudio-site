Private beta

Play our games before they are out

Some of the games we are still building can already be played in the browser as a private beta. Sign in with Google to request access; we approve requests by hand, usually within a day.

Coffee Street: Dalat Hills

A cozy game about a coffee cart on a misty pine hill. Brew and serve drinks for passers-by, then spend what you earn on tables, flowers, paths and staff as more of the hill opens up. The whole story plays through five chapters to a coffee festival on the hilltop, with an endless mode after it.

Status
Private beta, work in progress
Plays in
Desktop and mobile browsers
Request beta access (sign in with Google)Latest devlog

Godreign: War of Four

Four teams of three heroes on one map. Fight across a floating sky island full of minions and jungle monsters. In the beta you play against bots, with two modes: the classic four-team match and a new test mode, Centre Siege.

Status
Private beta, early and changing fast
Plays in
Desktop browsers and Android phones (landscape)
Request beta access (sign in with Google)Latest devlog

How it works

Open the beta link and sign in with Google. If you are not on the list yet, you will see a short note that your request is waiting. We approve requests by hand, usually within a day, and then the game opens with the same link.

Beta builds change often and can have bugs. Questions or bug reports: support@coworkgamestudio.com.
